# Keys Express

Keys Express is a fully mobile locksmith: Technicians drive to the Customer for car, home and business lock and key work. This glossary covers the public website and the Admin panel that runs it.

## People

**Customer**:
A person or company asking Keys Express for help, by phone or through the website.
_Avoid_: Client, user, lead

**Technician**:
A Keys Express locksmith who drives to the Customer and does the work.
_Avoid_: Tech, worker, locksmith (for the person)

**Admin**:
The one owner login that edits the website and reads Service Requests.
_Avoid_: User, account, admin account

## Work

**Service**:
One kind of job Keys Express offers, such as Smart Keys or Residential, shown as a card on the site.
_Avoid_: Product, category, offering

**Service Request**:
A Customer's request for help sent through the website's Request Service form.
_Avoid_: Lead, submission, inquiry, ticket, message

**Emergency**:
A Service Request the Customer marked "This is an emergency".
_Avoid_: Urgent, priority

**Status**:
Where a Service Request stands: New (nobody has acted on it yet), Contacted (Keys Express has reached the Customer) or Done (the job is finished or closed).
_Avoid_: State, stage

**Business Account**:
An ongoing arrangement where Keys Express handles the locks and keys for a company's buildings, vehicles or rental properties. It is set up by phone; it has no login and is not stored on the website.
_Avoid_: Account, commercial account, client account

## Place

**Service Area**:
The home city plus the Towns that Technicians travel to.
_Avoid_: Coverage, region, territory

**Town**:
One named community in the Service Area besides the home city.
_Avoid_: City, location, suburb

## Website

**Business Settings**:
The business facts the whole site shows: name, phone, email, Service Area, payment methods, license number and logo.
_Avoid_: Site settings, config

**Page Section**:
One block of text on a page, such as the About page's story or the home page's Emergency Help, that the Admin can edit.
_Avoid_: Block, widget, module

**Home Section**:
A Page Section on the home page. Unlike other Page Sections, the Admin can also hide it.
_Avoid_: Home block
